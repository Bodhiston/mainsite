About Me

Hello! I'm Bodhi Confer-Wood, an independent game developer and author with a passion for creating immersive worlds and compelling narratives.

With over 1 year of experience in game development, I create ethers in a myriad of genres that blend nostalgic aesthetics with modern gameplay mechanics.

When I'm not coding or designing games, I write fantasy novels and short stories to capture a secondary heartstring of my audience.

My mission is to create games and stories that transport my audience to vibrant worlds where they can escape, explore, and connect with memorable characters.
